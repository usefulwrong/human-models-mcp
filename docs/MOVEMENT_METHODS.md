# Movement and measurement methods

Pose development begins with the named exercise variant performed by real
people. Compare at least two visual sources and record the inspected views,
frames, access dates and observations in the scenario references. Identify
gaps in view or phase coverage. Separate visual observations, inferred 3D
geometry, numerical targets and experimental measurements.

Compare posture, support placement and joint orientation with the model's
degrees of freedom and source ranges. For flat palms, use the palm support
centres and the shoulder/chest floor projection. The fingertips, wrists and
palms have distinct landmarks. The phase review covers hand direction,
elbow path, depth, alignment and support motion. Preserve unsuccessful runs
and their acceptance records when the reference and model disagree.

For each new trajectory, record excitation, activation, signed transmission
force, length, velocity and mechanical work. Report bilateral pectoralis,
triceps and stabilizer paths by saved phase with native identifiers and
aggregation definitions. Inspect the matched start, bottom and return
frames. Model activation is a simulated signal. Normalized surface EMG,
biological muscle volume and clinical reference ranges require independent
measurement.
